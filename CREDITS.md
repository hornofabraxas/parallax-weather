## Digits

The digits are rendered offline from **Inter Display Black** (Inter 4.1,
<https://github.com/rsms/inter>), copyright 2016 The Inter Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (`art/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt`, shipped next to the font file in `art/fonts/`). The
watch ships pre-rendered digit maps, not the font.

## When you publish

- **Everywhere the face is distributed** (the Pebble app store listing, a GitHub release), credit
  the three licensed photographs with author, title, a link to the source, the licence name with a
  link to the licence, and a note that they were modified. One line each is enough, for example:
  "Rain texture adapted from 'Raindrops on the glass' by BogTar201213
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Raindrops_on_the_glass.jpg), CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)".
- **CC BY-SA 4.0** (partly cloudy, rain): the converted textures and the thumbnails that contain
  them must stay under CC BY-SA 4.0. That applies to those images only, not to the code.
- **CC BY 2.0** (cloudy): attribution and a licence link; no share-alike.
- **Public domain** (sun, storm, moon): no requirement; credit kept as a courtesy. NASA and NOAA
  imagery must not be used to imply their endorsement.
- **The code and the generated textures** (snow, fog) are MIT licensed ([LICENSE](LICENSE)); that
  does not change the terms of the photographs above.
